## Roles

| Action | Owner | Editor | Viewer |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Annotate | ✅ | ✅ | 👁️ view only |
| Upload / import | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Manage classes | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Invite members | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Change roles | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Delete workspace | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |

## Image locks

- Opening an image **locks it** to you, kept alive by a heartbeat, so two people never overwrite each other's work.
- Owners can **force-release** a stuck lock.

## Project management

Projects can be **renamed, duplicated, merged, moved** to another workspace, given a **cover photo**, or deleted. Upload batches can be **assigned** to individual members.

## Accounts

- Sign in with email and password (JWT sessions).
- **Password reset by email**, change password, edit profile and avatar, delete account.
- Personalisation: dark, light or custom accent theme, compact mode, box style and grid size.
